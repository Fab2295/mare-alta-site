The game

The scenery is the game.

Just distance, shells, the sun as a clock, and the pause. A shark fin shows up ahead as a warning.

Played with the iPhone in landscape

Every landing counts.

Perfect!

The most rewarding moment: splash, sparkle and combo. It lines up with the landing haptics.

Close call!

Bonus for skimming past the shark; the buoy winds the sun's clock back.

Crash!

A clear but comical hit: coral border, the jangada wobbles, you lose seconds of sun.

Collection and shop

Sails and jangadas.
