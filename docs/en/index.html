The game

The scenery is the game.

Hold to dive down the wave, let go to fly off the next one. A quick tap does a little hop. The sun is your clock: buoys and stops give you more time, and a fin up ahead warns you of the shark.

Played with the iPhone in landscape

Every landing counts.

Perfect!

Land right on the downslope of a wave: splash, sparkle, shells and a buzz from your iPhone.

Close call!

Skim past the shark and take the bonus. Every shark you dodge is counted at the end of the run.

Crash!

Hit the shark? The jangada wobbles and you lose seconds of sun.

At sea

There's always something coming.

Gulls

They show up at three heights after the first 900 m. Read their flight before you jump.

Golden buoy

Buoys give +3 s of sun. The golden one, rare and only after 5 km, gives +9 s.

Air time

Every second in the air turns into points when you splash down. Big air is worth +50.

Maré Alta!

Chain perfect landings and shells count double for a while.

Missions

Three missions per level. Levelling up unlocks new sails in the Collection.

Baião and the sea

Baião music and the sound of the sea, with separate effects and music volume in Settings.

Coast map

Collection

Sails and jangadas.

Chita and Cordel unlock as you level up; Renda de bilro and Azulejo with shells. Plus over 40 Shop sails in sets: Under the Sea, Animals of Brazil, Flags and Northeast States.

Free to play.

Ads only when you want them: +10 s of sun or double shells at the end of a run. And every month one Shop sail unlocks by watching 3 ads.
