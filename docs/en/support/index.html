Support

Questions about the game, purchases and settings.

Are ads mandatory?
No. They only show when you choose: at the end of a run, for +10 s of sun or double shells, and for the Sail of the month. "Play again" stays the main button.
What is the Sail of the month?
On the 1st of every month the game picks a Shop sail that unlocks by watching 3 ads. It shows at the top of the Collection and in the menu. Progress is saved: if the month ends first, you pick up where you left off when it comes back.
How do I get sails and jangadas?
In the Collection: Chita at level 2, Cordel at level 4, Renda de bilro for 300 shells and Azulejo for 500. Shop sails show a lock until the Shop opens.
How do I level up?
Each level has 3 missions, shown in the menu. Complete all three to level up.
Why does a run sometimes start in Jericoacoara?
The direction is drawn at random each run: from Porto de Galinhas towards Jericoacoara, or the way back. Each direction has its own record on the Map.
How do I change the language?
In Settings, choose Português, English or Español. On first launch the game follows your iPhone's language.
How do I adjust sound, music or haptics?
Settings has volume for Sound effects and for Music, and a switch for Haptics. While paused, the gear opens Settings.
Is there Game Center or in-app purchases?
Not yet. Game Center, the Shop and Restore purchases are coming in a future update.
