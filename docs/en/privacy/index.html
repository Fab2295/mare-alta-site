Privacy

Updated September 2026.

Maré Alta doesn't ask for an account, an email or a login. Your progress is stored on the iPhone itself.

What stays on the device

Distance, best run, shells, coast stretches, collection and settings (language, sound, music, haptics) are saved locally on the iPhone. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Game Center

If you connect Game Center in Settings, Apple receives your best run for leaderboards and achievements, under Apple's privacy policy. If you don't connect it, nothing is sent.

Purchases

The Jangadeiro Pass and shop items are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the game only receives the purchase confirmation, to unlock the item and let you restore it.

Rewarded ads

Ads are optional and only appear when you choose to watch one to get +10 s of sun or double shells. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device data under its own policy. If iOS asks for tracking permission, you can decline and keep playing.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, we don't build profiles, and we don't ask for access to photos, contacts, location or microphone.

Children

The game doesn't ask anyone for personal data, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
